Company overview

ZEECO is a Rishra-based supplier of industrial and power products to government departments, PSUs and industrial buyers, mainly through the GeM portal and e-tenders.

We source directly from manufacturers and dealers, supply at competitive prices, and are building towards an online industrial-supply marketplace.

Vision and mission

VISION

To build India's most trusted industrial and power supply platform - a marketplace for government and private, B2B and B2C buyers, where every genuine seller competes fairly and every buyer gets the best price.

MISSION

To give government and industrial buyers quality products at honest prices and on time, by sourcing directly from manufacturers - and to give small suppliers a fair, transparent route to market with healthy margins.

The problem and our solution

PROBLEM

  • Tenders, OEM authorisations and compliance are hard for new MSMEs
  • No single place to compare manufacturer rates
  • Stocking from manufacturers needs working capital small traders lack

SOLUTION

  • End-to-end tender handling: bid, compliance, quotation, invoice
  • Competing quotes from manufacturers, best value selected
  • Supplier ships direct to consignee - low stock risk

Revenue model

TRADING MARGIN

Bid price = manufacturer quote + logistics + ZEECO margin

ZEECO buys at the supplier's quoted rate and invoices the buyer at the awarded rate. The supplier ships direct to the consignee.

REVENUE STREAMS

  1. Government tenders and GeM orders
  2. Private industrial orders
  3. Future: marketplace commission and B2C sales
